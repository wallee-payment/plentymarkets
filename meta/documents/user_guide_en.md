# Connect Your Store to wallee

Connect your PlentyONE store to wallee and access a wide selection of modern payment methods through a single integration.
Choose from a wide range of local and global payment methods and use modern checkout options via payment page. The checkout can be customized to match your store and brand.

Key features include:

* PlentyONE PWA (Progressive Web App) support out of the box
* Seamless migration to PlentyONE PWA
* Creating your individualised invoice documents and packing slips
* 3D Secure payments
* Refunds directly from PlentyONE
* Support for multi-store setups
* Access to the wallee portal with dashboards, reports, and exports
* Omnichannel capabilities
* Automatic reminders via post with our pingen.com integration
* Automatically print documents via the cloud
* PCI DSS compliance
* And more

## Requirements

A wallee account is required to use the plugin. If you do not have an account yet, you can sign up for a test account.
The plugin can be installed through the PlentyONE marketplace.
For detailed information about installation, configuration, payment methods, refunds, customization, PWA support, and other features, please refer to the wallee documentation.
